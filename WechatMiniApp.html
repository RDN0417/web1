Wechat Mini App(微信小程序)

WeChat Mini Programs are "apps within apps" within the WeChat ecosystem, allowing users to directly access various services and features without downloading. They offer advantages such as instant loading and minimal memory usage, and can be accessed by scanning a QR code, searching within WeChat, or clicking a link shared by a friend. Mini Programs feature integration with WeChat Payment, e-commerce functionality, location-based services, social connectivity, and interaction with offline partners, significantly enhancing the user experience.
微信小程序是微信生态系统内的「应用内嵌应用」，允许用户无需下载即可直接使用各种服务和功能。 它们提供即时加载、占用内存少等优势，可以通过扫描二维码、在微信内搜索或点击好友分享的链接来访问。 小程序的特点包括集成微信支付、电商功能、地理位置服务、社交连接，以及与线下对象交互等，可大幅提升用户体验。
