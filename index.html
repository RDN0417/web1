RFID Solution

Mainly refers to the use of ultra-high frequency (UHF) radio frequency identification (RFID) technology to provide a set of solutions. This solution has wide application in many fields, especially in the logistics and supply chain industry. By using UHF RFID technology, it enables real-time tracking and inventory management of items or assets, improving logistics efficiency and reducing errors.
主要是指利用超高频（UHF）射频识别（RFID）技术提供一套解决方案。该解决方案在许多领域都有广泛的应用，特别是在物流和供应链行业。通过使用超高频RFID技术，它可以对物品或资产进行实时跟踪和库存管理，提高物流效率并减少错误。

RFID Handheld+APP

All assets (had RFID tags) can be operated offline stock take, stock-in and stock-out through the item ID / location, and the detected status can be exported. Undetected items can be found using by the locator.
所有资产（有RFID标签）都可以通过物品ID/位置进行离线盘点、入库和出库操作，检测到的状态可以导出。定位器可以使用找到未检测到的项目。

UWB Solution

Ultra-Wideband (UWB) is a short-range wireless communication technology that operates in the frequency range of 3.1 to 10.6 GHz, enabling indoor positioning with centimeter accuracy. It's commonly used in various industries such as mobile, automotive, industrial, and consumer IoT, due to its high data transmission capabilities and low power consumption.超宽带（UWB）是一种短程无线通信技术，工作在3.1至10.6 GHz的频率范围内，能够实现厘米级精度的室内定位。由于其高数据传输能力和低功耗，它通常用于移动、汽车、工业和消费物联网等各个行业。
